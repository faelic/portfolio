# Favour's engineering perspective and interests

The following passages express Favour Chukwuemerie Ututu's perspective, with owner-approved wording updates on 11 November 2026.

## Favour's engineering perspective — in his own words

I started my career as a frontend engineer, so I've always loved seeing beautiful user interfaces. But they have to work, too. I love an interface that looks good, feels easy to use, and loads quickly. I don't want to wait a minute for a page to load just to do something simple.

As I kept building, I got more curious about what happens beneath the interface. Security, especially, caught my attention. It could be frontend security, backend security, or AI security. I want to understand where things can go wrong, what makes a system vulnerable, and what it takes to protect it. That curiosity is also what got me interested in AI safety.

I value well-architected products and the engineers who build them. I have a lot of respect for engineers who would rather delay a release than compromise on security. For me, it's exciting to see a beautiful, functional interface sitting on top of a thoughtfully designed backend, with security taken seriously across the whole system.

That same thinking shapes how I approach AI safety and agent boundaries. I also care about the boundaries around it: what it can access, what actions it can take, what needs human approval, and how it behaves when something unexpected happens. I care about building systems that are useful without being careless about safety.

## Favour's interests — in his own words

I love coding. I enjoy working with LLMs, experimenting with what they can do, and exploring their potential in applications. I'm especially curious about what becomes possible as AI systems get better at using tools and handling more complex tasks.

And I love playing video games. I really do. It's one of my favourite things to do outside coding.
